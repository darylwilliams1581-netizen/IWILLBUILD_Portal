After shutter, tell them the truth in this order:

1. **On this phone now.** The JPEG is in the phone bucket (IndexedDB / queue). That is the photo. Signal does not have to be good.
2. **Not in the job library yet.** Cloudflare / Lens grid is the office copy. It appears after a successful POST.
3. **When the chip says synced**, then it is both: still may drop off the phone strip, and it belongs in the Cloudflare grid.

Never say “it uploaded” because the shutter clicked. Never hide a failed POST behind a fake grid tile. If `Couldn't upload` / clone errors happen, the file must stay in bucket 1 with Retry and Delete.

One grid that pretends to be both stores is how the photo vanishes. Two buckets is the paddock fence. Remember that.
